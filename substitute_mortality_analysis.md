### Summary of the 82 Plans That Left Substitute Mortality

- **38 switched to prescribed tables (46%)** -- These are still active DB plans that simply moved to Prescribed Separate (Code 2). The cost and complexity of maintaining substitute tables (experience studies, COVID adjustments, controlled group requirements) outweighed the benefit.
- **20 terminated (24%)** -- Filed a final Form 5500 return. One of these (RMI Titanium) also had a confirmed PRT transaction ($71.7M).
- **5 have PRT activity (6%)** -- No final filing yet, but Schedule H shows insurance company transfers. These appear to be winding down through annuity buy-outs.
- **6 merged into sibling plans (7%)** -- The plan's EIN still has other active plans filing in 2024, indicating corporate restructuring or plan consolidation.
- **4 are likely late filers (5%)** -- Still reported Code 3 in their 2023 filing; no 2024 filing yet but almost certainly still active (see below).
- **9 have unclear status (11%)** -- Stopped filing without a final filing indicator or PRT activity. Likely terminated or restructured outside our data window.
